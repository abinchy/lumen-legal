Contact

For app issues, billing questions, VPN connection problems, feedback, feature requests, or privacy questions, contact support at abinchy@hotmail.com.

Please include your app version, device model, iOS version, selected server region, and a short description of the issue. Do not send passwords, private keys, or sensitive browsing details.

VPN Connection Help

  • Check that your internet connection works without the VPN enabled.
  • Try another server region if the selected server is unavailable or slow.
  • Try switching protocol if your current network blocks one connection type.
  • Restart the app and reconnect if iOS reports a stale VPN state.
  • Confirm that VPN permissions are allowed in iOS Settings.
