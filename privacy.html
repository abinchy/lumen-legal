Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how VYPN, bundle identifier com.abinchy.lumen, handles information when you use the mobile application and related services.

VYPN is designed to provide VPN connectivity. A VPN service may need to process limited technical information to establish and maintain a secure connection.

Information We Process

Depending on the features you use and the services enabled in the app, we may process:

  • Connection information, such as selected server, protocol, connection status, latency, and timestamps.
  • Device and app diagnostics, such as app version, device type, crash data, and performance data.
  • Approximate network information, such as IP address, needed to provide VPN routing and abuse prevention.
  • Subscription status and purchase information provided by Apple for Premium features.
  • Advertising or analytics information if ads or analytics SDKs are enabled in the app.

VPN Traffic

We do not intentionally collect the contents of your browsing activity, such as the websites you visit, page contents, messages, or files transmitted through the VPN tunnel.

We may process limited technical connection data needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the VPN service.

How We Use Information

  • To provide and maintain VPN connectivity.
  • To select, refresh, and troubleshoot VPN servers and protocols.
  • To manage Premium subscription access.
  • To detect abuse, prevent fraud, and protect the service.
  • To improve app reliability, performance, and user experience.
  • To comply with legal obligations and App Store requirements.

Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services for app distribution, subscriptions, advertising, analytics, crash reporting, server infrastructure, or customer support. These providers may process information under their own privacy practices.

Apple processes App Store purchases and subscriptions according to Apple's privacy policy.

Data Retention

We keep information only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required by law, security, fraud prevention, billing, or dispute resolution needs.

Your Choices

  • You can stop VPN processing by disconnecting the VPN.
  • You can manage subscriptions through your Apple ID settings.
  • You can limit ad tracking and analytics through iOS privacy settings where available.
  • You can contact us to request privacy support or data deletion where applicable.

Children's Privacy

VYPN is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

International Use

Your information may be processed in countries other than where you live. We take reasonable steps to protect information according to this Privacy Policy.
